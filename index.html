      About FCE

      FCE (Functional Cognition & Execution) is a web-based tool that measures how your current cognitive performance compares to your own normal capacity — not to population averages.

      Instead of asking “How do you compare to others?”, FCE asks:

      “How are you functioning today compared to yourself?”

      This makes the results fairer, more meaningful, and more useful in real-world situations.

      What FCE measures

      FCE assesses multiple cognitive domains through short, focused tasks, such as:

      • Reaction speed (how quickly you respond)
      • Inhibitory control (your ability to act or not act appropriately)
      • Precision and motor control (accuracy of movement)

      Each domain is measured independently, because speed, control, and precision are not the same thing.

      Personal baselines — not population averages

      FCE works by building a personal baseline from several sessions performed when you are rested and functioning normally.

      All future checks are compared to your own baseline, not to age groups, norms, or other people.

      This matters because:

      • people differ naturally
      • devices differ (desktop vs mobile)
      • input methods differ (mouse vs touch)
      • context matters

      FCE is designed to respect those differences rather than ignore them.

      Context-aware by design

      Performance can vary significantly depending on:

      • device
      • input method
      • posture
      • fatigue
      • stress

      For this reason, FCE results are context-sensitive. Baselines apply to the same device and input method used to build them.

      This avoids false conclusions caused by environmental differences rather than true functional change.

      What FCE is not

      FCE is not:

      • a medical diagnosis
      • a clinical assessment
      • a legal judgment
      • a comparison against other people
      • a test of intelligence or worth

      FCE measures functional performance only, under standardized but simple conditions.

      Privacy and data

      All data is stored locally on your device using your browser’s storage.

      • No accounts
      • No tracking
      • No data is sent to a server
      • Clearing your browser data clears FCE data

      You remain in full control of your information.

      How to interpret results

      FCE results are intended as signals, not labels.

      • “Within normal range” means your performance aligns with your personal baseline.
      • “Below normal range” means performance differs from your usual capacity and may warrant rest, caution, or context-aware decisions.

      Results should always be interpreted alongside real-world factors such as sleep, workload, stress, and environment.

      Why FCE exists

      FCE was created to explore a simple idea:

      Functional performance matters more than assumptions.

      By focusing on function rather than substances, categories, or population norms, FCE aims to support fairer, clearer, and more humane decision-making.
