# FCE — Functional Cognition & Execution

FCE measures how your performance changes — not how you compare to others.

It compares your current performance to your own baseline, under consistent conditions.

It produces structured signals of change.
It does not diagnose, detect, or decide.

Interpretation remains human.

---

## What it is

FCE is a **baseline-relative measurement instrument** for observing changes in cognitive and execution performance over time.

It focuses on how a person performs relative to their own normal range, not population averages.

---

## What it measures

FCE measures execution-related signals through short, controlled tasks:

- Reaction time and variability  
- Inhibitory control (Go / No-Go)  
- Divided attention under dual demand  
- Motor precision and spatial accuracy  

These are measurable signals within the system.

---

## What it does not do

FCE does **not**:

- diagnose medical conditions  
- detect substances  
- determine fitness for driving or work  
- provide legal or safety decisions  
- explain causes  

FCE measures signals. It does not produce conclusions.

---

## How it works

FCE compares:

> current performance vs personal baseline

Sessions are structured into three modes:

- **Baseline** — establish normal performance  
- **Check** — compare current state  
- **Training** — practice only (no baseline impact)  

Evaluation is based on deviation from baseline.

---

## Interpretation limits

- Deviation thresholds (e.g. 1SD / 2SD) are **heuristic signals**, not calibrated indicators of real-world capability  
- FCE has **not been validated** against real-world performance outcomes  
- Short-task performance does not fully represent real-world behavior  

FCE provides structured signals, not definitive answers.

---

## Where it can be used

FCE may be useful in contexts such as:

- personal self-check before demanding tasks  
- recovery and rehabilitation support  
- fatigue and load monitoring  
- athletic readiness observation  
- exploratory research and discussion  

Use always requires human interpretation.

---

## Conditions for responsible use

FCE should only be used in ways that preserve:

- voluntary participation  
- informed consent  
- no automatic decisions  
- human review and interpretation  
- separation from enforcement systems  

If these conditions are not met, use is not aligned with the design.

---

## Position

FCE is best understood as:

- a structured self-monitoring instrument  
- a source of functional signals  
- a support for human judgment  

It is not a validated decision system.
